About the SDP

From biosignal to connected health solution.

Designed as a compact, practical learning experience, the programme moves from the fundamentals of biomedical signals to sensor interfacing, cloud logging and a demonstrable smart-health prototype.

⅁

Learn by building

Explore biomedical sensors and acquisition, signal conditioning, circuit simulation, PCB design and embedded boards through guided demonstrations and practical activities.

Connect data to decisions

What participants will explore

Skills to take beyond the workshop.

By the end of the SDP, participants will have followed a practical workflow from signal acquisition to a connected monitoring concept.

01 / ACQUIRE

Understand biosignals

Identify common biomedical signals and learn the basics of acquisition, artifacts, electrodes and signal conditioning.

02 / ENGINEER

Design & interface

Explore PCB workflows, embedded boards, sensor interfacing and common communication protocols.

03 / CONNECT

Visualise & demonstrate

Stream, log and visualise sensor data using software and cloud dashboards, then present a prototype concept.
